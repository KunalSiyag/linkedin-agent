# Growth playbook: @dazzbuilds.ai (IG Reels) · "dazz | Make with AI" (YT Shorts) · @dazzbuilds (Pinterest)
Faceless English AI-tools channel, one vertical video a day, global audience, near-zero budget. Goal: organic followers and impressions now, sponsorships and SaaS promotion later.
Researched 20 Oct 2026. Sources are cited inline. Lines tagged **[official]** come from Instagram, Meta, YouTube or Pinterest. Lines tagged **[practitioner]** come from third-party data or creator experience, so treat them as starting points to test.

---
## 0. The 10 rules that matter most
1. **Make it sendable.** Instagram ranks on predicted actions, including watch time, likes, comments and DM shares. Mosseri calls *sends per reach* "one of the most important signals". Every video needs one "I have to show this to someone" moment, like a wild result, a free alternative to a paid tool, or a hack that saves hours. [official] https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389/ · https://blog.recurpost.com/instagram-ranking-signals-published-vs-invented/
2. **Win the first 1–3 seconds.** Meta's Reels model predicts "how likely you are to watch less than three seconds". YouTube ranks on "% of viewers who chose to view" (Stayed to watch), average view duration and average % viewed. Open on the payoff, with no logo or intro. [official] https://blog.recurpost.com/instagram-ranking-signals-published-vs-invented/ · https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225
3. **Be unmistakably original.** Since 30 Apr 2026, Instagram doesn't recommend accounts that mostly repost. Since ~1 Oct 2026, YouTube cuts Shorts-feed reach for low-effort reuploads. Our own voiceover, script, motion graphics and screen recordings count as transformation. Pasting someone's demo clip with captions does not. [official] https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator/ · https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/ · https://www.androidauthority.com/youtube-shorts-promoting-original-content-reposts-3718668/
4. **Don't look like a template farm.** YouTube's July 2026 inauthentic-content rules demonetise "generic, repetitive, template-based" channels with little variation from video to video. Keep the brand template, but change the script structure, hook type, visuals and angle every time. [official] https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/
5. **No empty engagement bait; comment-keyword CTAs that deliver are our default.** Instagram won't recommend content that asks for engagement for its own sake ("tag a friend", "share this", "comment 🔥"). Keyword CTAs are fine when the DM actually delivers something, so ours ('Comment "JOBS" for the link') are always paired with an Instagram comment auto-DM that sends the repo link (Kunal, 20 Oct 2026). [official] https://socialschmuck.com/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/
6. **Use 3–5 specific hashtags (never more); keywords do the SEO work.** Instagram caps posts at 5 hashtags. Mosseri says hashtags help search but "don't increase your reach", and generic tags like #reels or #explore can hurt. Put real search phrases in the first caption line, the on-screen text and the voiceover. [official] https://www.theverge.com/news/847739/instagram-hashtag-spam-limit · https://indianexpress.com/article/technology/social/posting-on-instagram-you-can-now-use-only-five-hashtags-per-post-and-reel-10429929/
7. **Upload clean, native files on every platform.** Instagram makes "low-resolution or watermarked reels, reels that are muted or contain borders, reels that are majority text" less visible. Never cross-post a file carrying another app's watermark, and don't bake copyrighted music into the file. [official] https://blog.recurpost.com/instagram-ranking-signals-published-vs-invented/
8. **Run Trial Reels as real A/B tests.** Make two variants of one idea that differ meaningfully, for example the hook. Mosseri (19 Sep 2026 Q&A): reposting the same trial is detected and its reach is limited. [official] https://www.thereelstars.com/tech/adam-mosseri-reveals-instagram-trial-reels-rule-that-could-limit-your-reach/ · https://marcus.marketing/blog/ig-ceo-qa-2026-09-19/
9. **Turn on Instagram's AI Reel translation (dubbing + lip sync).** Mosseri says it raises reach because the reel can be shown to speakers of the translated languages. Use YouTube auto-dubbing where it's available. This is free global reach. [official] https://marcus.marketing/blog/ig-ceo-qa-2026-09-19/
10. **Be consistent, then judge on rates.** Post daily at a fixed slot, timed 30–60 min before the audience peak (Mosseri, via Buffer). Judge each video on rate metrics (sends/reach, watch %, follows per 1k reach), not raw views. Followers still drive a large share of reach (Mosseri, Sep 2026). [official+practitioner] https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/ · https://marcus.marketing/blog/ig-ceo-qa-2026-09-19/

## 1. Angle and topic selection (daily, ~20 min)
**Where to look (all free):**
- GitHub Trending (daily, by language) https://github.com/trending, plus the star-velocity tools Trendshift https://trendshift.io/ and OSS Insight (`https://api.ossinsight.io/v1/trends/repos/?period=past_24_hours`) https://ossinsight.io/blog/introducing-trending-page and RepoAlerts https://repoalerts.com/ to catch repos *before* they peak. Ref: https://daily.dev/blog/github-trending-alternatives-spot-rising-repos-early/
- Hacker News front page and Show HN, Product Hunt top of day, the Hugging Face trending Spaces and models page, and r/LocalLLaMA, r/ChatGPT and r/ClaudeAI (top posts of the day).
- X: `search_posts_all` with queries like `("open source" OR github) (agent OR claude OR cursor OR "local llm") min_faves:500`, last 24h (max 25 results, be frugal). Also lab and tool launch accounts.
- Competitor scan: look at the top 9 most-viewed reels this week on @notmy.bug (~32K followers, sells sponsored reels: https://shoutcart.com/influencer/technology-influencer-43108), @ai.byprince and similar pages. A topic that works for two of them = proven demand. Do the same topic with a fresher angle and our own demo, and never reupload their clip.

**Score each candidate 0–3 per criterion; ship the top scorer (≥12/18):**
| Criterion | 3 = |
|---|---|
| Sendability | Viewer instantly thinks of a friend ("you need this") |
| Mass relevance | Non-devs get it (jobs, money, saving time, free alt to paid) |
| Visual demo | We can screen-record a clear before/after in ≤10 s |
| Freshness | <72 h old, or rising fast (stars/day climbing) |
| Proof | A hard number or result (stars, "69 apps → 20 interviews") |
| Low friction | Free, runs locally or in-browser, no waitlist |

**Angle bank (rotate, never two days in a row):** free alternative to a paid tool · "this replaces X" · a real person's result story · a hidden feature or hack · a three-tool workflow stack · "stop doing X manually" · "I tested it so you don't have to" (honest verdict) · a contrarian or myth-bust · a news explainer in 30 s.
**Mix per week:** about 4 trending repos or tools, 2 workflows or hacks, and 1 evergreen "best free AI tools for X" (great on Pinterest and YouTube search). From day ~30, let 1 in 7 cover our own SaaS (proupiqr.in, pintdownload.app) or Gumroad items, framed as a useful tool, not an ad.
**Verify everything** against the primary source (README, GitHub API, official post) before scripting. Drop anything we can't verify.

## 2. Hook formulas (line 1 lands in ≤1.5 s, with on-screen text matching the voiceover)
1. **Result first:** "This repo got a laid-off dev hired in 3 weeks."
2. **Free swap:** "Stop paying $20/month for [tool]. This one's free and open source."
3. **Replace:** "This one GitHub repo replaces your entire [job/stack]."
4. **Curiosity gap:** "Developers are quietly using this to [outcome]…"
5. **Number + timeframe:** "40,000 GitHub stars in 4 days. Here's why."
6. **Stop doing X:** "If you still write cover letters by hand, stop."
7. **Secret / hidden:** "Claude Code has a feature almost nobody uses."
8. **Unbelievable demo:** "I typed one sentence and it built the whole app."
9. **Contrarian:** "ChatGPT isn't the best AI for this. This is."
10. **Identity callout:** "Every freelancer needs this AI in their toolkit."
11. **Before/after:** "3 hours of work → 3 minutes. Watch."
12. **Series hook:** "day 14 of finding AI tools that feel illegal to know."
13. **News urgency:** "OpenAI just dropped this, and it changes [X]."
14. **Question:** "What if your AI could apply to jobs while you sleep?"

Rules: put a concrete noun or number in the first 5 words, deliver what the title promises in the first 10 s (no bait-and-switch), and keep it true. A misleading hook raises swipe-aways and "not interested" taps, which YouTube explicitly measures (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225). In the reference style (notmy.bug / ai.byprince), the hook plays over very fast shots with lowercase serif or typewriter text typing on. **Our rule (Kunal, 20 Oct 2026): project-first.** The actual repo/UI/result is on screen from frame 1 (by 1.5 s, hard limit 2.0 s, enforced by `set_storyboard` and `qa_render`); abstract clips only as 0.3–0.5 s flashes between project shots, never 3–4 s of abstract footage first.

## 3. Script structure and length
- **Length:** 25–40 s (65–110 words), so we can tighten as watch % data comes in. Hard ceiling of 60 s. Instagram has said it doesn't recommend reels longer than 90 s to non-followers (https://socialschmuck.com/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/). Pinterest video sweet spot is 6–15 s (https://getpinrank.com/blog/pinterest-video-pins), so cut a short version for it.
- **Beats:** Hook (0–2 s) → "day N" card plus what it is (2–6 s) → 2–3 concrete steps on a real screen recording (6–25 s) → proof or surprising number (25–32 s) → payoff plus soft CTA (last 3–5 s).
- **Re-hook every 6–8 s** with a visual change, zoom, highlight box, SFX or a new line of text.
- **Loop-friendly ending:** the last line should flow back into the first frame (rewatches add watch time).
- Vary the structure across videos (story-led, list-led, demo-led, myth-bust) so the channel never reads as template output (see the YouTube rule in §0.4).
- ElevenLabs v3 voice: one consistent voice is the brand. Use emotion tags sparingly, with energy on the hook and the proof.

## 4. On-screen text
- The hook text (5–9 words) sits in the upper-middle safe zone, readable muted. Keep it clear of the bottom ~20% and the right-side buttons. [practitioner] https://www.bereel.pro/blog/reels-first-frame-mute-first-captions
- Burned-in word-synced captions for every line; most people watch muted, especially on Pinterest.
- Say and show the **keyword** ("free AI resume builder", "Claude Code agent"). Instagram and Google read captions, speech and on-screen text for search. [practitioner] https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-search/ · https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo/
- Don't let text dominate a frame. "Majority-text" reels are down-ranked [official, see §0.7]. Show motion and demo, not a slide.
- A persistent small "day N" label builds series recognition.

## 5. Captions, hashtags, keywords (Instagram SEO)
- **Line 1 (before "more"):** keyword phrase plus hook, e.g. "free AI job search agent that writes your CV 🤖". It's the most visible text and it's indexed.
- **Body:** 3–6 short lines: what it is → 3 steps → proof → where to get it ("repo: owner/name"). Instagram captions don't make links clickable, so give the exact repo name.
- **Hashtags: 3–5, all specific** (#claudecode #aitools #github #opensource #jobsearch). Never use #reels, #explore or #fyp (Instagram warns these can hurt). Cap of 5. https://indianexpress.com/article/technology/social/posting-on-instagram-you-can-now-use-only-five-hashtags-per-post-and-reel-10429929/
- Public professional accounts' Reels can be indexed by Google, so write captions like a mini landing page. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expanding-google-search-indexing-public-posts-more-regions/752891/
- Profile SEO: put a keyword in the Name field ("dazz | AI tools & repos daily") and keep the bio to one promise.
- Sponsored posts: use Instagram's Paid partnership label and #ad.

## 6. Covers
- The IG profile grid crops to **3:4**, so keep cover text centred inside the 1080×1440 middle area. The cover says the *outcome* in ≤5 words ("AI got him hired"), with large serif type in the paper-label style and a consistent look for the grid.
- Pick a frame with high contrast and an obvious subject (UI with a highlight box, or the result).
- YouTube Shorts: choose the frame in the YouTube app if the API doesn't set it. Pinterest: pick a strong cover frame, since it's what shows before autoplay. https://getpinrank.com/blog/pinterest-video-pins
- Once the series has 9+ posts, make covers look like a series (same layout, changing number and keyword).

## 7. CTAs that drive saves, shares, follows, comments without bait
- **Earn the share, don't ask for it.** Build the reel around a moment people want to forward. Explicit "share this" or "tag a friend" risks losing recommendations [official, §0.5].
- **Follow CTA (best for growth):** tie it to the series: "follow, day 15 is even crazier" / "I post one of these every day". Say it once, at the end.
- **Save CTA:** frame the value as reference: "save this for the next time you job-hunt". Make the content worth saving (steps, commands, a list of 3 tools).
- **Comment-keyword CTA (default):** 'Comment "<WORD>" for the link' (one topic word, e.g. JOBS/REPO), paired with an Instagram comment auto-DM (e.g. ManyChat / Meta's own automation) that DMs the repo link. Comments are a ranking signal and the DM thread is a follow-up channel. Set the keyword in the auto-DM tool before posting and also put the repo name in the caption. In the land look the CTA scene shows the keyword as the mega background word (`cta.comment_word`). Open questions ("what should I test for day 15?") remain a good pinned-comment prompt. https://socialschmuck.com/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/ · https://aubrium.com/blog/comment-x-and-ill-dm-you
- **Link in bio** for our products and Gumroad (one link page, free).
- Pinterest explicitly rewards save and follow CTAs: "the more your ideas are saved, the more you will show up". https://create.pinterest.com/en-gb/product-features/how-to-create-pins/

## 8. Cadence and posting times (global English audience; times in IST)
- **Cadence:** 1 Reel or Short per day, every day, on the same platform-native file pipeline. Buffer finds that reach per post rises with frequency; 3–5/week is the floor. [practitioner] https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/ Add 1–2 IG Stories a day (repost the reel plus a poll) per Mosseri's suggested weekly mix (same source).
- **Benchmarks** (Buffer, 9.6M posts, local time): evenings 6–11 PM are strongest, and Wednesday/Thursday are the best days. Thursday mornings at 7–9 AM are an exception. Friday and Saturday are weakest, and 1–5 AM is dead. Post 30–60 min before the audience is online.
- **Default slot (start here): 9:30 PM IST daily.** That's 12:00 PM ET until Oct 31 (11:00 AM ET after US DST ends on 1 Nov 2026), 4–5 PM in the UK, and the late-evening peak in India. It also falls in Kunal's waking hours for the first-hour replies.
- **Test slot (Trial Reels / week 2): 7:00 AM IST.** That's 9:30 PM ET / 6:30 PM PT before 1 Nov (8:30 PM ET / 5:30 PM PT after), the US evening peak, plus India's morning commute.
- Put the strongest topic of the week on **Tue–Thu**. Use Fri/Sat for evergreen or lighter topics.
- After 14 days, check IG Insights → Followers → *Most active times* and *Top countries*. If the US is over 35% of the audience, move the main slot to 7:00 AM IST.
- **YouTube Shorts:** publish at the same time as IG. Shorts distribution is driven by performance more than recency, so timing matters less.
- **Pinterest:** 6:30–8:30 AM IST (US evenings) and weekends. Pins are evergreen and keep resurfacing. https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pin-performance-and-distribution

## 9. Trial Reels
- What it is: a reel shown first to non-followers only. Insights arrive after ~24 h in Profile → Reels → *Drafts and trial reels*. You can share manually, or auto-share if it performs well within 72 h. Professional account required (some sources say 1,000+ followers). Scheduling Trial Reels was added in April 2026. https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/ · https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/what-are-instagram-trial-reels · https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-allows-creators-to-schedule-trial-reels/816549/
- The **API supports it**: create a REELS container with `trial_params: {graduation_strategy: "MANUAL" | "SS_PERFORMANCE"}`. https://www.sume.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels-api-graduation-strategy (check whether Composio's Instagram tool passes `trial_params`, otherwise post trials from the app).
- **How we use it:** 2–3×/week, test one variable at a time (hook, length, music vs no music, cover text) as 2 *meaningfully different* variants. Compare trial against trial on sends/reach and average watch %, not trial against regular reels. Graduate the winner. **Never re-post an identical file** (Mosseri, 19 Sep 2026). https://www.thereelstars.com/tech/adam-mosseri-reveals-instagram-trial-reels-rule-that-could-limit-your-reach/
- Also use trials for risky new formats, so a flop doesn't dent follower-feed signals.

## 10. Cross-posting rules
- Render a **master without any platform UI or watermark**, then export a separate file per platform: re-encode (new container and metadata), 1080×1920, 30/60 fps, H.264, loudness around −14 LUFS. Never upload a file downloaded from another platform.
- Keep the text safe zones for all three platforms (Pinterest player controls cover the top and bottom; image safe zones are top 270 px and bottom 790 px). https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/review-pin-specs
- **Music:** don't bake in copyrighted tracks. Ship a voice + SFX + royalty-free bed version for the API, and a no-music version that Kunal can post with an in-app trending track when it's worth it. On YouTube, Shorts' licensed library can be added in-app.
- Write platform-specific copy: an IG caption, a YT title + description, a Pinterest title + description + link. Never paste the same text everywhere.
- Upload natively on each platform (Composio API). Space the uploads ~5–30 min apart; there's no need to stagger days.

## 11. YouTube Shorts: title, description, SEO
- **Title (≤60 chars):** keyword first, then the hook. "Free AI Job Search Agent That Got Him Hired". Add `#shorts` only in the description or title end. Match real autocomplete phrasing (type the topic into YouTube search). Keep it accurate. https://miraflow.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-seo-2026-how-to-rank-shorts-in-search
- **Description:** line 1 restates the keyword. Then 2–3 lines of what it is and how to get it, the repo or official link, sources, and 3–5 hashtags (#shorts + broad + niche + specific). The first 3 hashtags show above the title. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658 · https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449
- Group Shorts into playlists ("AI tools daily", "Claude Code tools", "Free AI alternatives") for search and binge.
- Use the **related video** link to point a Short at the best-performing Short or a future long-form video.
- Watch **Engaged views**, **Stayed to watch** and **Average % viewed**. Since 24 Aug 2026, "views" count from the first frame, so raw views are inflated. https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/ · https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281
- YPP-safe: original script and insight in every video, and no repetitive templated clones. AI voice is allowed, but realistic AI personas are not allowed on finance, health or legal topics. https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/

## 12. Pinterest pin SEO
- Pinterest is a search engine, so research keywords in the Pinterest search bar's autocomplete and on Pinterest Trends (https://trends.pinterest.com/).
- **Title (≤100 chars):** keyword first ("Free AI Resume Builder: Open-Source Job Search Agent"). **Description (≤800 chars; Pinterest uses it for relevance):** 2–3 natural sentences with long-tail keywords plus a save CTA. https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/review-pin-specs
- Add **up to 10 topic tags** per pin, a **destination link** (repo, Gumroad or product page) and a **specific board** with a keyworded title and description ("Free AI Tools for Job Seekers", "Open Source AI Agents", "AI Productivity Hacks", "Claude Code & Cursor Tips"). https://create.pinterest.com/en-gb/product-features/how-to-create-pins/
- Make the video silent-first: start with the result in the first second, use 6–15 s cuts, and pick a strong cover. Static infographic pins ("5 free AI tools for X") often out-click video, so add 2–3 static pins a week from each video's frames. https://getpinrank.com/blog/pinterest-video-pins
- Gen-AI labels apply to image pins detected as AI-generated. Prefer real UI screenshots and screen recordings. https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/gen-ai-labels

## 13. Engagement routine (≈15 min/day)
- **First 60 min after posting:** reply to every comment with a real answer (a question back keeps the thread going). Pin the most useful comment, ideally the repo link or name or a correction, or our own comment with "repo: owner/name + one tip". Buffer reports replying lifted engagement ~21%. [practitioner] https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/
- Deleting spam or troll comments **doesn't hurt reach** (Mosseri, Sep 2026). https://marcus.marketing/blog/ig-ceo-qa-2026-09-19/
- Share the reel to Stories with a poll or quiz sticker (followers see Stories). Reply to DMs.
- 10 min/day: leave thoughtful comments on 5–10 bigger AI-tool creators' new posts (early, adds value). Engagement pods or "comment networks" make content ineligible for recommendations. https://blog.recurpost.com/instagram-ranking-signals-published-vs-invented/
- Turn the best comment questions into tomorrow's video ("you asked…"). That's free topic research.
- Tag the repo author or tool on X/IG when allowed; they often reshare, which is free distribution.

## 14. Metrics to track (log per video in `notes/metrics.csv` at 24 h and 7 d) and thresholds
| Metric (where) | Target | If below for 5+ videos |
|---|---|---|
| IG skip rate / 3-s hold (Insights) | skip <50% | change hook type, first frame and on-screen text |
| IG avg watch time as % of length | ≥60% for a 30 s reel | cut length 20%, add re-hooks, remove the slow middle |
| IG **sends per reach** (shares ÷ reach) | ≥1% (great ≥3%) | pick more sendable angles (§1 score) |
| IG saves per reach | ≥1% | add steps, commands, lists worth saving |
| IG follows per 1k reach | ≥3 (great ≥10) | stronger series identity + follow CTA, clearer profile promise |
| IG non-follower reach share | ≥70% early on | originality or bait issues; check Account Status |
| YT **Stayed to watch** | ≥70% | hook / first frame |
| YT avg % viewed | ≥80% (loops can push >100%) | shorten, loop ending |
| YT subs per 1k engaged views | ≥2 | series CTA, playlists |
| Pinterest saves and outbound clicks per 1k impressions | saves ≥5, clicks ≥3 | keywords, cover, board fit |
These targets are our working heuristics, not platform-published numbers. Re-baseline after 30 videos using our own median.
- **Iteration loop:** each Sunday, rank the week's 7 videos by sends/reach × watch %. Do the top format's topic family more and kill the bottom two formats. Change only 1–2 variables per week, and test them via Trial Reels.
- Check **IG Account Status** (Settings → Account status → "Can your content be recommended?") weekly.

## 15. Things that get reach suppressed
- Reposting or aggregating others' videos without meaningful transformation (IG, all formats since 30 Apr 2026; YT Shorts since ~1 Oct 2026). Speed changes, borders, captions and credits alone don't count. [official, §0.3]
- Watermarks from TikTok, CapCut or other apps; low resolution; borders or letterboxing; muted reels; majority-text reels. [official, §0.7]
- Engagement bait without real delivery ("tag 3 friends", "share if…", a comment keyword whose auto-DM isn't live); engagement pods; buying followers or likes. Comment-keyword CTAs with a working auto-DM are fine and are our default. [official, §0.5, §13]
- Generic or irrelevant hashtags (#reels, #explore), hashtag stuffing (capped at 5 now). [official, §0.6]
- Reels over 90 s (not recommended to non-followers on IG). [official, §3]
- Copyrighted music baked into the file (muted or blocked; muted reels are down-ranked).
- Posting the same trial reel repeatedly. [official, §9]
- Templated, repetitive AI videos with no original insight (YouTube YPP; also hurts satisfaction signals). [official, §0.4]
- Misleading hooks or titles → "not interested" and swipe-aways; distressing or emotionally manipulative content. [official] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225
- Community-guideline strikes or recommendation-guideline violations (check Account Status). Recommendations only go to public accounts.

## 16. Reference-style recipe (from our breakdown of @notmy.bug and @ai.byprince reels, Oct 2026)
- 0–5 s: 4–8 rapid abstract shots with a lowercase serif or typewriter hook typing on → paper-tear or halftone wipe → a "Day N" card.
- Middle: one long landscape screen recording of the tool, with eased pans and zooms, a large cursor, highlight boxes and SFX on clicks.
- End: CTA on torn paper strips plus a sticker (cat). Trending music sits low under an emotive voiceover.
- Our edge over them: verified numbers, an honest "heads-up" line (cost/limits), sharper keyword captions, Trial Reel testing, translations, and three platforms from one master.

**SFX density:** the music stems carry 0.4-1.1 onsets/s including the beat, so SFX are sparse and land on visual events (wipes, clicks, highlights), not on every cut. Our default auto-SFX density: ~20 placements in 34 s (≈0.6/s), on wipes, clicks, first highlights, stickers and the CTA. Levels are now high enough to read clearly over the music.

**Baked into the dazz template defaults (lib/look_dazz.py, lib/sfx_auto.py, lib/render.py, generate_music auto):**
1. Project-first hook (`project_first`, `project_by` 2.0 s; QA check).
2. Abstract shots only as 0.3-0.5 s flashes (warning otherwise).
3. 5. Zoom punches on bar downbeats in screen scenes (`beat_punch`).
6. SFX ~+9 dB louder (punchy transients, limiter on the master instead of dynamic loudnorm).
